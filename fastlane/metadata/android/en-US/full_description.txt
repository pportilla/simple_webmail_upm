Manage your Universidad Politécnica de Madrid email from a native Android app. UPM Mail supports existing @upm.es, @alumnos.upm.es and @externos.upm.es accounts.

UPM Mail is an independent, unofficial app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Universidad Politécnica de Madrid. Use an existing UPM mailbox or configure a compatible TLS IMAP/SMTP account. The app does not create a provider account.

TRY SAMPLE MAIL
Explore a fictional mailbox without mailbox credentials. Sample mail stays on your device; sending, replies and provider reports are simulated locally and never delivered to real addresses. Try local sample arrivals and notifications. Other connected real accounts keep their normal network activity.

FOR AGES 13 AND OLDER
Use a mailbox you are authorized to access and meet your provider's account requirements.

BLOCK AND REPORT
Block unwanted sender addresses on this device and manage blocked addresses in Settings. Write a report to your mail provider's official abuse or safety address, preview it and choose when to send. Original correspondence is not attached automatically. Local blocking does not change server delivery or other apps.

YOUR MAIL, ORGANISED
Switch between multiple accounts and browse Inbox, Sent, Drafts, Archive, Trash, Spam and your own folders. Read conversations, star messages and use swipe or multi-select actions to archive, move, delete or mark mail as read. Undo recent actions and cancel a queued send during its undo period.

WRITE AND SHARE
Reply, reply to all or forward messages. Add Cc/Bcc recipients, a signature and attachments. Recipient suggestions use your mail history and, if you grant permission, your device contacts. Save drafts, open or save attachments, and share text or files into a new email. An outbox keeps queued messages for sending when a connection is available.

FIND OLDER MESSAGES
Search cached mail and indexed message headers across your folders. Search the mail server for additional results or message text, and narrow results by folder, unread status, stars or attachments.

CHOOSE HOW MAIL ARRIVES
For connected real mailboxes, choose push checking through a direct IMAP connection, periodic checks every 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or checking when you open the app. Push is off by default. Real push uses an ongoing connection-status notification and can be turned off in Settings. Sample arrivals refresh locally without a persistent service. Delivery timing depends on connectivity, server availability and Android background restrictions.

READ YOUR WAY
Remote images are blocked until you allow them. Choose light or dark appearance, optional system colours, and an English or Spanish interface. Open mailto links and print messages.

For real mail, the app connects directly to the configured servers using IMAP and SMTP. It does not create a UPM account or replace the University's account-management service.
